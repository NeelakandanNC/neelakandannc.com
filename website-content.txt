BUILDER (№ 04 — Builder Log · 9 Ventures · 1 Active)
================================================================

Born builder.
Every venture is a lesson. Every failure is data. What follows is a
working log — the journey, unredacted.

№ 01 — SciTech Knowledge  [Foundation]
Learned the foundations through YouTube channels. Self-taught across
science and technology, building a base of knowledge that fuels everything
after.
Lesson: Self-education is the most powerful investment you can make.

№ 02 — ModernClother  [Lesson Learned]
First e-commerce venture — tried to sell apparel online. Pricing was too
high and I had no understanding of sales and marketing at the time.
Lesson: Product-market fit matters more than the product itself. Pricing
and distribution are everything.

№ 03 — MediPro Solutions  [NABH Agency]
Created an agency to help hospitals obtain NABH certification. Built the
operations, outreach, and service model from scratch.
Link: https://1mediprosolutions.wixsite.com/medi-pro-solutions
Lesson: B2B services require deep domain expertise and trust-building over
time.

№ 04 — Flaura  [Paused]
Did beta testing and created a deck. Stopped due to lack of funds and a gap
in innovation that I couldn't bridge at the time.
Lesson: Timing and capital are just as important as the idea.

№ 05 — LumoScript  [Stopped]
Just started — a new venture in the early stages. No clients yet so the
work is still nascent, but the foundation is being laid.
Lesson: Every big thing starts small. The key is to start.

№ 06 — Arthhive  [Stopped — 9 mo]
Worked on this for 9 months. Due to lack of time and competing priorities,
we decided to pause the project.
Lesson: Time is the scarcest resource. Choose your battles wisely.

№ 07 — Ween  [Stopped]
MVP is ready and the launch was planned. The product was taking shape but
is currently stopped.
Lesson: Ship it. Iterate. Don't wait for perfect.

№ 08 — repoforthat.dev  [Closed]
A platform to discover the right repo for any problem. Shipped the curation
engine and developer tooling surface, then sunset the venture to refocus.
Link: https://repoforthat.dev
Lesson: Build what you wish existed — and know when to close the chapter.

№ 09 — Agentronics  [Active]
Currently active — building at the intersection of autonomous AI agents and
electronics. Agentic systems that reason about hardware, sensors, and the
physical world.
Lesson: The next frontier is agents that touch atoms, not just bits.
